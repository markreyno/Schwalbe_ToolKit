App missing after uninstall?

Android sometimes keeps an old “installed” flag after you removed the app. Clear it, then install again:

  1. Settings → Apps — uninstall Schwalbe ToolKit or remove it from the home screen if it still appears.
  2. Chrome → Site settings for this desktop address — tap Clear & reset or delete site data.
  3. Return here and use Install app again, or open the warehouse hub in Chrome.

Add to home screen

When your browser offers it, tap Install. You can also use the button below.

  1. Tap the Share button in Safari.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Schwalbe ToolKit from your home screen.
